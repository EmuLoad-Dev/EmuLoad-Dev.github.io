About the game

Sudoku is a calm, focused take on the classic number puzzle. Choose a 9×9 or 6×6 board, pick Easy, Normal or Hard, and work through a ladder of 365 levels. Every puzzle is generated to have a unique solution, so logic always gets you to the answer.

What’s inside

Two board sizes

Classic 9×9 with 3×3 boxes, plus a quicker 6×6 board with 2×3 boxes.

2,190 puzzles

Six ladders — two board sizes across three difficulties — each with 365 levels that unlock in order.

Pencil notes

Jot candidates in a cell and narrow them down as the board opens up.

Hints

Watch a short rewarded ad to reveal a helpful digit when you are stuck.

Solution walkthrough

Step through the reasoning of a puzzle after you have cleared it.

Progress that stays

Cleared levels and an unfinished board are saved on your device, so you can pick up where you left off.

How it plays

  • Every level has one unique solution and is generated from a fixed seed, so a level is always the same puzzle.
  • Levels unlock in order: clear level N to open level N + 1.
  • A game allows up to three mistakes before the board is closed.
  • The puzzles themselves run fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for ads and hints.
  • No account, no sign-in, and no in-app purchases.

Support

Where is my progress saved?

Cleared levels, the current unfinished puzzle and your settings are stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to sign in to.

I reinstalled the app and my progress is gone.

Progress lives only on the device. Deleting the app also deletes its saved data, and reinstalling starts fresh. There is no cloud backup for progress at the moment.

The Hint button does not respond.

Hints are earned by watching a rewarded ad, so they need a working internet connection and an available ad. If no ad can be loaded, the button stays inactive — try again later or continue with pencil notes.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Puzzle generation and play are fully offline. Only ads, and therefore hints, require a connection.

Why does the app ask for tracking permission?

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. iOS may ask whether the app can track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. Declining is fine — the game still works and ads are simply less personalized. You can change the choice later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I reset my progress?

Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all locally stored progress. There is currently no in-app reset button.
