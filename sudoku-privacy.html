Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how the Sudoku mobile game for iOS (“the App”) handles information. The App is published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

The App has no account, no sign-in and no profile. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. If you email us for support, we receive the address you wrote from and whatever you choose to include in the message.

Information stored on your device

The App keeps a small amount of gameplay data locally on your iPhone:

  • Which levels you have cleared on each ladder.
  • The unfinished puzzle, including the board and pencil notes.
  • Your selections and settings, such as board size and difficulty.

This data stays in the App’s local storage. We do not receive it, it is not synced to a server, and deleting the App deletes it.

Advertising

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, including an app-open ad, occasional interstitial ads, and rewarded ads that unlock hints. To deliver, measure and limit those ads, Google and its partners may process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) when you allow tracking.
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived.
  • Ad interactions, such as impressions, clicks and completions.
  • Device type, operating system version and diagnostic information.

This data is handled under Google’s own privacy terms. We do not receive your personal data from Google, and we do not sell personal information. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your tracking choice

On first launch, iOS may show an App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether the App may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you allow it, your IDFA may be used for personalized advertising. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent in some regions

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows a consent message through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your advertising choices there, or through the Privacy Options entry in the App when it is offered. You can also manage personalized ads through Google Ad Settings.

Sharing

We do not share data with third parties except through the advertising service described above. Google acts as an independent provider for that service. No analytics, crash-reporting, social or cloud-storage SDK is used.

Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete the App. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google’s policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because we hold almost no data ourselves, requests about advertising data may need to be directed to Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question and we will help where we can.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App or its services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact
